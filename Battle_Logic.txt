==============================================
          MAGE BATTLE - BATTLE LOGIC
==============================================

==============================================
1. GAME STRUCTURE
==============================================

PLAYERS:
  - Total: 4 players (Player 0, 1, 2, 3)
  - Teams: 2v2 format
    * Team 0 (Team A): Player 0 & Player 2
    * Team 1 (Team B): Player 1 & Player 3
  - Turn Order: Sequential (0 � 1 � 2 � 3 � 0...)

PLAYER POSITION:
  - Players sit in a circle: P0 � P1 � P2 � P3 � (back to P0)
  - Left Neighbor: Previous player in turn order (wraps around)
  - Right Neighbor: Next player in turn order (wraps around)
  - Example: For P0, left is P3, right is P1

WIN CONDITION:
  - Eliminate both players of the opposing team


==============================================
2. PLAYER STATS
==============================================

HP (HEALTH POINTS):
  - Initial: 50 HP
  - Max: 50 HP
  - If HP reaches 0: Player dies

SHIELD:
  - Initial: 0
  - No maximum limit
  - Damage Absorption: Shield absorbs first, remaining damage does not go to HP
  - Damage Absorption does not apply on true damage.

DEATH & REVIVAL:
  - When HP = 0: Player dies immediately
  - Death Effects:
    * All cards in hand are discarded
    * All buffs/debuffs are removed
    * Cannot act during death
  - Death Counter: Tracks turns dead (increments on each of player's turns)
  - Revival: After 2 turns dead, player revives with 50% HP (25 HP)


==============================================
3. ATTRIBUTES & PROFICIENCY
==============================================

ATTRIBUTE TYPES:
  - Main Attributes: Fire (F), Wood (W), Thunder (T), Water (Wa)
  - Sub Attributes: Wind (Wi), Poison (Po)

ATTRIBUTE LEVELS:
  - Range: 0 to 5
  - Players allocate 1 attribute point per turn
  - Cannot exceed level 5 in any single attribute
  - If every element reached level 5, skip the attribute allocation.

ATTRIBUTE PROFICIENCY (PASSIVE BONUSES):

FIRE (F):
  - Lv3: All attribute bolts +1 damage
  - Lv5: At turn start, deal 1 skill damage to all enemies (bypasses Wood Lv5 reduction)

WOOD (W):
  - Lv3: When using Wood spell:
    * Cost: -1 HP (DIRECT, bypasses shield entirely)
    * Benefit: +1 Shield
  - Lv5: Self and teammate receive -1 spell damage from all spell damages

THUNDER (T):
  - Lv3: Thunder spells +1 damage
  - Lv5: Thunder spells +2 additional damage (total +3 with Lv3)

WATER (Wa):
  - Lv3: When using Water attribute spell, heal self +1 HP
  - Lv5: When using Water attribute spell, also heal teammate +1 HP

WIND (Wi):
  - Lv2: Targets hit by Wind spells cannot heal or gain shield this round
  - Lv5: Wind spells can target any player (not restricted to default targeting)

POISON (Po):
  - Lv2: Targets hit by Poison spells must play card before allocating attributes next turn (Confuse debuff)
  - Lv5: Targets hit by Poison spells can only use attribute bolts next turn (Silent debuff)


==============================================
4. TURN STRUCTURE
==============================================

TURN PHASES (in order):

1. TURN START:
   - Death counter increments (if dead)
   - Revival check (if dead for 2 turns)
   - Fire Lv5 effect triggers (if applicable)
   - Health Drain effect triggers (if applicable)
   - Regeneration effect triggers (if applicable)
   - Burning Out effect triggers (consumes 1 Fire attribute point)

2. ALLOCATE ATTRIBUTE:
   - Current player allocates 1 attribute point
   - Choose from: Fire, Wood, Thunder, Water, Wind, Poison
   - Cannot exceed level 5

3. PLAY CARD:
   - Current player must play exactly 1 card:
     Option A: Cast a spell (top or bottom side of card)
     Option B: Use attribute bolt with the card
   - Cannot skip this phase

4. DRAW CARD:
   - Current player draws 1 card from deck
   - If deck is empty, shuffle discard pile into deck

5. TURN END:
   - Buff/debuff durations tick down
   - Move to next player


==============================================
5. CARDS & SPELLS
==============================================

CARD STRUCTURE:
  - Each card has 1 or 2 spell sides
  - Top Spell: Always present
  - Bottom Spell: Optional (some cards are single-sided)

SPELL REQUIREMENTS:
  - Format: "F2W1" means Fire Lv2 AND Wood Lv1 required
  - Must meet ALL requirements to cast
  - Requirements are checked at casting time

SPELL EFFECTS:
  - Damage: Deal X damage to target(s)
  - Heal: Restore X HP to target(s)
  - Shield: Grant X shield to target(s)
  - Buffs/Debuffs: Apply status effects
  - Special: Various unique effects

ATTRIBUTE BOLT:
  - Count as spell. It means it deal spell damage
  - Requires: 1 any card + attribute level > 0
  - Damage: Equal to attribute level
  - Target: Left neighbor (previous player in turn order) only
  - Cannot target dead players
  - Enchantment: Considered to have the bolt's attribute for proficiency bonuses


==============================================
6. DAMAGE CALCULATION
==============================================

DAMAGE TYPES:
  - Spell damage: The damage caused by spells.
  - Skill damage: The damage caused by skills.
  - Direct damage: The damage speficied on description.

DAMAGE FLOW:
  1. Calculate Base Damage
  2. Apply Caster's Proficiency Bonuses:
     - Fire Lv3: +1 to if the spell is attribute bolt.
     - Thunder Lv3: +1 to thunder spells
     - Thunder Lv5: +2 additional thunder spells
     - Burning Out buff: +5 to fire spells
  3. Apply Target's Damage Reduction:
     - Wood Lv5: -1 from all spell damage
     - Guard Wood Carving buff: -4 from spell and skill damage (decrements hit counter)
  4. Final Damage = max(0, damage after all modifiers)
  5. Apply damage to target

SHIELD MECHANICS:
  - Decrease Shield equals to the damage
  - Shield has no maximum value
  - Shield can not block direct damage (Wood Lv3 proficiency)
  - The surplus damage from the shield DOES NOT deal to HP.
  - Example: 50HP, 7 shield, took 5 damage =>50HP, 2 shield
  - Example: 50HP, 7 shield, took 10 damage =>50HP, 0 shield

WOOD LV3 HP COST (SPECIAL CASE):
  - The -1 HP cost is DIRECT HP reduction
  - It completely bypasses shield
  - Applied BEFORE the spell effect resolves
  - Then +1 shield is granted
  - Example: 45 HP, 1 shield, trigger Lv3 wood proficiency => 44 HP, 2 shield


==============================================
7. TARGETING RULES
==============================================

DEFAULT TARGETING (for spells if not specified):
  - MUST target furthest alived enemy
  - Cannot target: Dead players

SPELL CARD TARGETING:
  - Varies by spell's TargetPool specification
  - Common patterns:
    * Default: Right neighbor (furthest enemy in turn order)
    * Self: The caster
    * Ally: Caster or teammate
    * Enemy: Any living enemy
    * All: Multiple targets
  - Wind Lv5: Overrides restrictions, allows free target selection

FURTHEST ALIVED ENEMY:
  - Definition: Alived enemy with maximum distance going forward in turn order
  - Example (for P0):
    * P1 (distance 1)
    * P2 (distance 2) - teammate
    * P3 (distance 3)
    * Furthest Alived enemy is P3
  - Example (for P0):
    * P1 (distance 1)
    * P2 (distance 2) - teammate
    * P3 (distance 3) - dead
    * Furthest Alived enemy is P1


==============================================
8. CHARACTER TYPES
==============================================

FLAME POISON (Fire/Poison Mage):
  - Initial Attributes: Fire 2, Poison 1
  - Main: Fire
  - Sub: Poison
  - Liberation (Burning Out):
    * Requirement: Fire 4, Poison 2
    * Effect: Fire damage +5, consume 1 Fire per turn
    * Duration: Permanent (until Fire runs out)

WOOD WIND (Wood/Wind Mage):
  - Initial Attributes: Wood 2, Wind 1
  - Main: Wood
  - Sub: Wind
  - Liberation (Guard Wood Carving):
    * Requirement: Wood 4, Wind 2
    * Effect: Self and teammate take -4 damage
    * Duration: Until hit 3 times

THUNDER POISON (Thunder/Poison Mage):
  - Initial Attributes: Thunder 2, Poison 1
  - Main: Thunder
  - Sub: Poison
  - Liberation (Chain Lightning):
    * Requirement: Thunder 4, Poison 2
    * Effect: Deal 10 damage to all other players
    * Type: Instant (no duration)

WATER WIND (Water/Wind Mage):
  - Initial Attributes: Water 2, Wind 1
  - Main: Water
  - Sub: Wind
  - Liberation (Hurricane Eye):
    * Requirement: Water 4, Wind 2
    * Effect: Regenerate 7 HP per turn
    * Duration: 4 turns


==============================================
9. BUFFS & DEBUFFS
==============================================

POSITIVE BUFFS:

Immune:
  - Effect: Immune to damage and negative effects
  - Duration: Varies

Invincible:
  - Effect: Immune to damage and negative effects
  - Duration: Until next player completes their turn

Regeneration:
  - Effect: Heal 7 HP at turn start
  - Duration: Turns-based

Burning Out:
  - Effect: Fire damage +5
  - Cost: Consume 1 Fire attribute at turn start
  - Duration: Permanent (until Fire = 0)

Guard Wood Carving:
  - Effect: Take -4 damage from all sources
  - Counter: Decrements on each hit
  - Duration: Until hit 3 times

Health Drain:
  - Effect: Deal 1 damage to target, heal self 1 HP at turn start
  - Duration: Varies


NEGATIVE DEBUFFS:

Paralysis:
  - Effect: Cannot take any actions

Seal:
  - Effect: Cannot use liberation skill

Silent:
  - Effect: Can only use attribute bolts (no spell cards)

Master Disable:
  - Effect: Attribute proficiency bonuses do not apply

Defense Invalidation:
  - Effect: Cannot heal HP or gain shield

Confuse:
  - Effect: Must play card BEFORE allocating attributes

Health Drain Target:
  - Effect: Take 1 damage at drainer's turn start


==============================================
10. DURATION TYPES
==============================================

Permanent:
  - Lasts indefinitely (until manually removed or condition met)

Turns (N):
  - Lasts for N of the affected player's turns
  - Decrements at turn end

UntilHit (N):
  - Lasts until hit N times
  - Decrements on each damage instance

UntilNextPlayer:
  - Lasts until the next player completes their turn


==============================================
11. SPECIAL MECHANICS
==============================================

DECK & HAND:
  - Starting Hand: 5 cards per player
  - Draw: 1 card per turn (at Draw Card phase)
  - Deck Size: 60 cards total (shared deck)
  - Discard Pile: Used cards go here
  - Reshuffle: When deck empty, shuffle discard pile into deck

LIBERATION SKILLS:
  - Each character has 1 unique liberation skill
  - Requirement: Specific attribute levels
  - Usage: Once per game
  - Replaces "Play Card" phase when used
  - Still draw a card after using liberation

PROFICIENCY STACKING:
  - Multiple proficiency bonuses stack additively
  - Example: Fire Lv3 (+1) + Thunder Lv3 (+1) + Thunder Lv5 (+2) = +4 total for Thunder damage

SPELL ENCHANTMENTS:
  - A spell is "enchanted" with attributes if it requires those attributes
  - Example: "F2W1" spell is enchanted with Fire and Wood
  - Enchantments determine which proficiency bonuses apply
  - Wood Lv3 cost triggers for ANY Wood-enchanted spell/bolt

13. COMBAT EXAMPLE
==============================================

SCENARIO: Player 0 (Wood Lv3) attacks Player 3

Initial State:
  - Player 0: 45 HP, 1 Shield, Wood Lv3
  - Player 3: 50 HP, 0 Shield

Action: Player 0 uses Wood attribute bolt (Lv3)
  - Card cost: 1 card from hand (discarded)
  - Wood Lv3 cost triggers:
    * Player 0: -1 HP (DIRECT, bypasses shield) � 44 HP
    * Player 0: +1 Shield � 2 Shield
  - Base damage: 3 (Wood level)
  - Proficiency: None applicable
  - Target: Player 3 (left neighbor)
  - Damage reduction: None
  - Final damage to P3: 3 HP

Final State:
  - Player 0: 44 HP, 2 Shield
  - Player 3: 47 HP, 0 Shield


==============================================
14. IMPORTANT CLARIFICATIONS
==============================================

1. WOOD LV3 HP COST:
   - The -1 HP cost is NOT affected by shield
   - The -1 HP cost is NOT affected by Wood Lv5 damage reduction
   - The -1 HP cost is NOT affected by any damage reduction effects
   - It is a DIRECT HP cost, not damage

2. ATTRIBUTE BOLT TARGETING:
   - ALWAYS targets left neighbor (previous in turn order)
   - This is NOT the same as "attack right"
   - Turn order: P0 � P1 � P2 � P3 � P0
   - P0's left neighbor = P3
   - P1's left neighbor = P0

3. DAMAGE VS HP COST:
   - Damage: Affected by shield, damage reduction, immunities
   - HP Cost: Direct HP reduction, bypasses ALL protections

4. PROFICIENCY TIMING:
   - Wood Lv3 cost: Triggers BEFORE spell effect
   - Other proficiencies: Applied during damage calculation
   - Fire Lv5: Triggers at turn START, before any actions

5. DEATH TIMING:
   - Death is checked immediately after HP reaches 0
   - Can die from Wood Lv3 cost before spell resolves
   - Can die from any damage source

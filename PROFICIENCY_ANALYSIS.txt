2. HOW SPELLS TRIGGER PROFICIENCIES:
   Flow: play_spell_card() -> execute_spell_effect() -> apply_effect() -> handler functions
   
   Key mechanism: "enchantments" vector
   - Extracted from spell.requirements: vec![Fire, Thunder, etc]
   - Passed through effect chain to enable selective proficiency application
   - Different effects match different proficiencies:
     * Damage effects (Fire Lv3, Thunder Lv3/5)
     * Heal effects (Water Lv3/5)
     * Wood Lv3 special: applied in execute_spell_effect BEFORE other effects
   
   Code locations:
   - Spell execution: game.rs lines 388-425
   - Effect routing: game.rs lines 429-745
   - Damage proficiencies: game.rs lines 748-798
   - Heal proficiencies: game.rs lines 800-840

3. ATTRIBUTE BOLTS IMPLEMENTATION:
   File: game.rs lines 209-261, play_attribute_bolt() method
   
   Process:
   1. Any card can be discarded to cast bolt
   2. Attribute level must be >= 1
   3. Target: furthest alive enemy (auto)
   4. Enchantment created manually: vec![attr_type]
   5. Effect used: IncreaseDamage(0) - damage = attr_level + proficiency bonus
   
   Why IncreaseDamage(0)?
   - Reuses damage proficiency system elegantly
   - Fire Lv3 adds +1
   - Thunder Lv3 adds +1, Lv5 adds +2
   - Same proficiency handling as spell cards

4. SPELLS vs ATTRIBUTE BOLTS - PROFICIENCY DIFFERENCES:

   IDENTICAL BEHAVIOR:
   - Fire proficiencies (Lv3 damage boost)
   - Thunder proficiencies (Lv3/5 damage boost)
   - All passive proficiencies (Fire Lv5, Wood Lv5, Wind Lv2/5, Poison Lv2/5)

   DIFFERENT BEHAVIOR:
   - Wood Lv3: Spells trigger it (HP cost + shield), Bolts don't (no shield cost in IncreaseDamage)
   - Water Lv3/5: Spells trigger it (heal bonus), Bolts don't (no healing in IncreaseDamage)

   Reason for differences:
   - Attribute bolts use IncreaseDamage(0) effect
   - IncreaseDamage doesn't trigger healing or shield proficiencies
   - Only damage proficiencies apply

5. ACTIVE vs PASSIVE PROFICIENCIES:

   ACTIVE (during spell/bolt casting):
   - Fire Lv3: +1 damage (if Fire in enchantments)
   - Thunder Lv3/5: +1/+3 total damage (if Thunder in enchantments)
   - Water Lv3/5: +1 healing bonuses (if Water in enchantments + Heal effect)
   - Wood Lv3: -1 HP + 1 shield (if Wood in spell)

   PASSIVE (automatic, outside spell execution):
   - Fire Lv5: 1 skill damage to all enemies at turn start
   - Wood Lv5: -1 spell damage reduction in take_damage()
   - Wind Lv2/5: Target debuffs (can't heal/shield, can't play cards, etc)
   - Poison Lv2/5: Target debuffs (play card first, only bolts)

6. DAMAGE TYPE CLASSIFICATION:
   File: damage.rs (DamageType enum)
   
   Three types affect proficiency application:
   - Spell: Affected by Wood Lv5 (-1), Guard Wood Carving (-4), hit by shields
   - Skill: NOT affected by Wood Lv5, affected by Guard Wood Carving (-4), hit by shields
   - Direct: Bypasses shields, not affected by any reductions
   
   Implementation: player.rs take_damage() applies reductions based on damage type

8. DESIGN PATTERN:
   The system uses "enchantments" as a selective proficiency trigger mechanism:
   - Enchantments = attribute types from spell requirements
   - Effect handlers receive enchantments
   - Handlers match enchantments to proficiencies
   - This allows:
     * Multiple proficiencies per spell (multi-attribute spells)
     * Identical proficiency application for spells and bolts
     * Easy addition of new proficiencies
     * No spell-specific hardcoding (except Wood Lv3 special case)

9. WOOD LV3 EXCEPTION:
   Only proficiency applied BEFORE other effects in execute_spell_effect()
   Reason: HP cost must apply before healing effects
   Code: game.rs lines 402-415

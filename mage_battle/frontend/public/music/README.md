# Background Music Folder

Place background music files here for different game screens.

## Required Music Files

### Lobby Music (lobby.mp3)
- Plays during the lobby and waiting room screens
- Should be looping
- Recommended: Calm, ambient music
- Format: MP3

### Gameplay Music (gameplay.mp3)
- Plays during active gameplay
- Should be looping
- Recommended: Energetic, battle-themed music
- Format: MP3

## Audio Specifications

- **Format**: MP3
- **Bitrate**: 128-192 kbps recommended (balance between quality and file size)
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1 kHz
- **Channels**: Stereo or Mono
- **Length**: 2-5 minutes (will loop)

## Volume Control

Players can adjust the background music volume using the volume control popup (🔊 音量設定 button):
- Background Music volume is adjustable from 0% to 100%
- Default: 30%
- SFX volume control is also available (for future sound effects)

## Adding New Music

1. Place your MP3 files in this folder with the correct names:
   - `lobby.mp3`
   - `gameplay.mp3`

2. The music will automatically play based on the current screen:
   - **Lobby/Waiting Room**: lobby.mp3
   - **Game**: gameplay.mp3

3. Music will automatically loop and transition when switching between screens

## Notes

- Music autoplay may be blocked by browsers until the user interacts with the page
- Keep file sizes reasonable (< 5MB per file) for faster loading
- Consider copyright/licensing when selecting music
